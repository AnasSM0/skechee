Privacy Policy

Skechee Publisher (private, single-user app) · Last updated: 19 November 2026

Skechee Publisher is a private tool used only by its owner to publish the owner's own videos and images to the owner's own YouTube channel, Facebook Page, Instagram professional account and TikTok account.

Data collected

The app accesses only the accounts of its owner, using permissions the owner grants through Google OAuth, Facebook Login and TikTok Login Kit. It stores access tokens, the connected accounts' names and IDs, the owner's own media files, captions, schedules, and the IDs and links of published posts. From TikTok it uses only the connected account's basic profile information (display name and ID) and the permission to upload the owner's videos to that account.

Where data is stored

All data is stored locally on the owner's computer; access tokens are stored encrypted. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics. This website itself uses no cookies, trackers or analytics.

Third parties

Content is sent only to Google (YouTube), Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and TikTok through their official APIs, at the owner's request, in order to publish it on the owner's own accounts.

Retention and deletion

The owner can delete all data at any time by removing the app's local database and revoking access at Google account permissions, Facebook business integrations and, in the TikTok app, Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions. To request deletion of any data, contact the app owner at the email address listed on the developer app page.

Children

The app is not directed at children and is used by a single adult owner only.

Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
