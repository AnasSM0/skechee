Skechee Publisher (private, single-user app) · Last updated: 19 November 2026

1. What this app is

Skechee Publisher is a private scheduling tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It uploads the owner's own videos and captions to the owner's own YouTube channel, Facebook Page, Instagram account and TikTok account at times the owner chooses.

2. Who may use it

The app is used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public: there is no sign-up, no other users, and it never connects to or acts on anyone else's accounts.

3. Content and responsibility

The owner is solely responsible for the content published through the app and confirms that they own or have the rights to it. Nothing is published without the owner's own approval in the app, and for TikTok the owner reviews and posts each video from their TikTok drafts.

Use of the app is subject to the terms and policies of each connected platform, including the TikTok Terms of Service and TikTok Developer Guidelines, the YouTube Terms of Service, and Meta's Terms. The app uses only those platforms' official APIs and the permissions the owner grants.

5. Access and revocation

Access to each platform can be revoked at any time from that platform's account settings. Revoking access stops the app from publishing to that account.
