Skechee Publisher

A private publishing tool for the Skechee channel

Skechee Publisher is a personal scheduling tool used only by the creator of Skechee, an animated slice-of-life channel. It runs on the creator's own computer and uploads the creator's own videos – full episodes and short parts of the series The Days In Between – to the creator's own YouTube channel, Facebook Page, Instagram account and TikTok account at scheduled times.

It is not a public service: no one else can sign up or log in, and it does not access anyone else's accounts or data.
